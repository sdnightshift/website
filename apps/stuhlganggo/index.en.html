Diary & Self-tracking

StuhlgangGo

A discreet stool diary based on the Bristol stool scale – for everyone who wants to keep an eye on their everyday routine. One tap on any of the seven types logs an entry, the history reveals your patterns, and whenever you need one you generate a tidy report in seconds. Everything stays on your device.

What you can expect

One tap, one entry

All seven Bristol types sit right on the home screen. A single tap is enough – the time fills in automatically. If you want to record more, add urgency, stress level, colour, findings and a short note.

Discreet home screen widget

Seven numbers on your home screen, nothing else – no title, no counter, nothing that gives away what it tracks. A tap opens the app with that type ready, so you capture the details while you still remember them.

History & type trend

The history groups your entries by day, and the trend chart plots the type over time – with stool colour, stress level and findings as layers. Patterns that get lost in everyday life become visible.

A report to take with you

Pick a range – 7, 30, 90 days or custom – and export a print-ready PDF: summary, type distribution, trend and every entry in one table. The same data is available as CSV for your own analysis.

100% local

No cloud, no account, no analytics. Your entries live in a database on your device and nowhere else – the app has no network layer at all. Data only leaves the device through an export you share yourself.

Protected & adaptable

Lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your device PIN if you want to. Plus light and dark mode, German and English, and a switch that wipes all of your data whenever you choose.

Free & Pro

Logging, history and the trend chart are free forever and ad-free. Only the export – PDF report and CSV raw data – is the Pro feature, unlocked with a single one-time payment. No subscription.
