Diary & Self-tracking

StuhlgangGo

StuhlgangGo is a discreet stool diary in development. Record entries using the seven types of the Bristol stool scale, add optional notes and keep track of your history. Your diary entries stay on your device, and you choose when to share a report.

In development. The features described here reflect the planned scope.

Planned features

One tap, one entry

All seven Bristol types sit right on the home screen. A single tap is enough – the time fills in automatically. If you want to record more, add urgency, stress level, colour, findings and a short note.

Discreet home screen widget

Seven numbers on your home screen, nothing else – no title, no counter, nothing that gives away what it tracks. A tap opens the app with that type ready, so you capture the details while you still remember them.

History & type trend

The history groups your entries by day, and the trend chart plots the type over time – with stool colour, stress level and findings as layers. Patterns that get lost in everyday life become visible.

A report to take with you

Pick a range – 7, 30, 90 days or custom – and export a print-ready PDF: summary, type distribution, trend and every entry in one table. The same data is available as CSV for your own analysis.

100% local

No cloud, no account, no analytics. Your entries live in a database on your device and nowhere else – the app has no network layer at all. Data only leaves the device through an export you share yourself.

Protected & adaptable

Lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your device PIN if you want to. Plus light and dark mode, German and English, and a switch that wipes all of your data whenever you choose.

Free & Pro

Logging, history and the trend chart are free forever and ad-free. Only the export – PDF report and CSV raw data – is the Pro feature, unlocked with a single one-time payment. No subscription.

Questions about StuhlgangGo

Features, availability and privacy at a glance.

Is StuhlgangGo available yet?

StuhlgangGo is currently in development. The features described here reflect the planned scope. Once the app is released, the relevant store link will appear on this page.

How does a stool diary entry work?

Choose one of the seven Bristol stool scale types. The time is filled in automatically. Optionally add urgency, stress level, colour, findings and a note. A discreet home screen widget is planned to open the app with the selected type ready.

Are my diary entries stored online?

Entries stay in a local database on your device. The diary is planned without a user account, cloud synchronisation or usage analytics. A PDF or CSV file leaves your device when you export and share it yourself. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I create a report from my history?

Planned PDF reports cover 7, 30 or 90 days, or a custom date range. They include a summary, type distribution, trend and a table of entries. CSV export is planned for your own analysis.

Which features are planned to be free?

Logging, history and the trend chart are planned to remain free and ad-free. PDF and CSV exports are planned as Pro features unlocked with a one-time purchase, without a subscription. The purchase screen will show the exact offer before you buy.

What can I use the records for?

StuhlgangGo helps you keep your own observations organised and share them in a report when needed. The app does not diagnose conditions or provide a medical assessment of entries. See the terms of service for its intended use.

Legal & Privacy

StuhlgangGo is purely a diary app and processes your entries exclusively on your own device. Here you'll find the specific terms of use and privacy policy for StuhlgangGo.
