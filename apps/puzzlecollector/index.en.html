Puzzle Collector

Puzzle Collector is an app in development for your jigsaw puzzle collection. Scan the barcode on the box, keep a wish list and use the logbook to record when you finished each puzzle.

Preparing for release. The store link will appear after launch.

Your collection at a glance

Collection, logbook and exports

Quick capture

Scan a barcode and import matching product details from the online catalog. Photos and on-device text recognition help fill in missing details. Premium also lets you capture multiple puzzles with batch scanning.

A collection with structure

Manage wish list, owned and sold puzzles, and track completed designs separately. Add storage locations, notes and details for boxes containing several puzzles.

Your puzzle logbook

Record completed puzzles in your logbook with a date. Add another entry when you complete a design again, and optionally enter a duration manually.

Puzzles and pieces at a glance

See how your collection grows, how many pieces you have completed and when you have been most active — compact, clear and easy to read.

Flexible exports

Share individual puzzles as images, text or PDFs and create sales text. Premium adds collection import and export using Excel files in XLSX format and custom text templates.

Local backups

Save collection details, images, logbook entries and settings in a local backup file and restore them for free. No Premium required. Optional iCloud sync keeps your collection up to date across supported Apple devices.

Start free, expand with Premium

Store up to 25 puzzles in total for free, including wish list and sold entries. Premium removes the collection entry limit and adds batch scanning, Excel import and export, and custom export templates. Depending on the store offering, choose a monthly subscription, annual subscription or one-time purchase. Creating and restoring local backups is free without Premium. Questions about Puzzle Collector

Can I scan barcodes without Premium?

Yes, standard barcode lookup in the online catalogue is planned for the free version. If the catalogue has no match, you can add the puzzle manually. Premium includes optional AI lookup with separate consent, subject to a monthly quota shared across the app.

Do wish list and sold puzzles count towards the free limit?

Yes. The planned free limit of 25 applies to all saved puzzles, including those on your wish list and those you’ve sold. Deleting an entry frees up a slot. Local backups are free.

Can I still edit my collection when my Premium subscription ends?

Yes. You can still edit saved puzzles after your subscription ends. Adding new puzzles is subject to the free limit. See the terms of service for details.
