Your collection at a glance

Everything your collection needs

Less list maintenance, more clarity — with tools built around the way puzzlers actually collect and solve.

Quick capture

Scan a barcode and import matching product details from the online catalog. Photos and on-device text recognition help fill in missing details. Premium also lets you capture multiple puzzles with batch scanning.

A collection with structure

Manage wish list, owned and sold puzzles, and track completed designs separately. Add storage locations, notes and details for boxes containing several puzzles.

Your puzzle logbook

Record completed puzzles in your logbook with a date. Add another entry when you complete a design again, and optionally enter a duration manually.

Motivating statistics

See how your collection grows, how many pieces you have completed and when you have been most active — compact, clear and easy to read.

Flexible exports

Share individual puzzles as images, text or PDFs and create sales text. Premium adds collection import and export using Excel files in XLSX format and custom text templates.

Local backups

Save collection details, images, logbook entries and settings in a local backup file and restore them for free. No Premium required. Optional iCloud sync keeps your collection up to date across supported Apple devices.

Start free, expand with Premium

Store up to 25 puzzles in total for free, including wish list and sold entries. Premium removes the collection entry limit and adds batch scanning, Excel import and export, and custom export templates. Depending on the store offering, choose a monthly subscription, annual subscription or one-time Lifetime purchase. Creating and restoring local backups is free without Premium. Questions about Puzzle Collector

Features, availability and privacy at a glance.

Is Puzzle Collector available yet?

Puzzle Collector is being prepared for release. The relevant store link will appear here after launch. Until then, the app is not publicly available.

How can I organise my puzzle collection?

Add photos, scan barcodes and use on-device text recognition for details on the box. Import matching product information from the online catalog. With active Premium and your separate consent, optional AI search using OpenAI can provide editable suggestions after a catalog miss. Manual entry remains available.

Can I track solving times?

Record completed puzzles with a date and an optional duration entered manually. Add further logbook entries when you complete a design again. Statistics show your collection, completed pieces and activity over time.

Where is my data stored and how can I back it up?

Your collection and images are initially stored locally. Depending on use, the online catalog, voluntary contributions, store purchases and optional services process data outside your device. AI search starts only after your express consent and can be turned off under Settings → About Puzzle Collector. iCloud sync, analytics and crash reports are also optional. See the privacy policy for details.

Which exports are planned?

Share individual puzzles as images, text or PDFs, or export images with sales text. Create and restore local backups for free. Premium also offers Excel import and export in XLSX format and custom export templates.

How is free access planned to work?

Store 25 puzzles in total for free, including wish list and sold entries. Depending on the store offering, Premium is available as a monthly subscription, annual subscription or one-time Lifetime purchase. Existing entries remain editable after a subscription ends. The offers and conditions shown in the app apply.
