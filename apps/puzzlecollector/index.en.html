Puzzle Collector

Puzzle Collector is an app in development for managing your puzzle collection and solving times. From the first photo of the box to the final piece, keep your collection, wish list, sessions and statistics together — with no user account and your collection data stored locally on your device.

In development. The features described here reflect the planned scope.

Everything your collection needs

Less list maintenance, more clarity — with tools built around the way puzzlers actually collect and solve.

Quick capture

Photograph a puzzle and its box, scan the barcode or capture printed details with on-device text recognition. Add the title, brand, series, artist, dimensions and article number whenever you like.

A collection with structure

Mark puzzles as wish list, owned or sold, record their storage location and status, and manage multiple copies of the same design in one shared view.

Track every session

Start, pause and finish solving sessions right from a puzzle. Daily activity and notes preserve the story of every attempt.

Motivating statistics

See how your collection grows, how many pieces you have completed and when you have been most active — compact, clear and easy to read.

Flexible exports

Share an individual puzzle as a PDF or sale sheet and export your collection as CSV. Custom text templates put exactly the details you need into every export.

Local backups

Back up metadata, images, solving sessions and settings in a portable file and restore them when needed. You decide where the file is stored.

Start free, unlock forever

Store up to 10 of your own puzzles in the free collection at the same time. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks an unlimited collection — no subscription.

Questions about Puzzle Collector

Features, availability and privacy at a glance.

Is Puzzle Collector available yet?

Puzzle Collector is currently in development. The features described here reflect the planned scope. Once the app is released, the relevant store link will appear on this page.

How can I organise my puzzle collection?

Planned capture tools include photos of puzzles and boxes, barcode scanning and on-device text recognition. Record details such as title, brand, piece count, artist, storage location and condition. Organise puzzles as wish list, owned or sold, and manage multiple copies of the same design together.

Can I track solving times?

Start, pause and finish sessions directly from a puzzle. Daily activity and notes add context to each attempt. Statistics give you an overview of your collection, completed pieces and activity.

Where is my data stored and how can I back it up?

Collection data and images are processed locally on your device. A portable backup file is planned for saving and restoring metadata, images, solving sessions and settings. External services may be used for optional analytics and in-app purchases; see the privacy policy for details.

Which exports are planned?

Share individual puzzles as PDFs or sale sheets, and export your collection as CSV. Custom text templates let you choose which details appear in an export.

How is free access planned to work?

The free collection is planned to hold up to 10 of your own puzzles at a time. A single one-time in-app purchase is planned to unlock an unlimited collection permanently, without a subscription. The offer shown at release will provide the final details.

Legal & privacy

Your puzzle data and images are processed locally. External services are used only for analytics you choose to enable and for in-app purchases. Find every detail here.
