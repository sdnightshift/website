App Privacy Policy

Puzzle Collector – Last updated: 2 November 2026

Puzzle Collector initially stores your collection locally. This policy explains when the online catalog, AI research, voluntary contributions, iCloud, purchases and optional diagnostics process data outside your device.

1. Controller and scope

The controller for Puzzle Collector is Sebastian Wolfgang Dichmann, SD Nightshift Studio, Am Ziegelstadel 20, 87700 Memmingen, Germany. Email: sdnightshiftstudio@gmail.com. See our Legal Notice for further details.

This policy covers the app. Visiting this website is also covered by the Website Privacy Policy.

2. Local collection and technical identifiers

The app does not require registration with your name, email address or password. Your collection is initially stored in the protected app area on your device: puzzle titles, brand, series, artist, piece count, dimensions, article number, barcode, ownership and solving status, storage location, notes, colours, dates, images, solving times and logbook entries. Settings and export templates are also stored locally.

External processing depends on the features you use: catalog lookups, AI research, voluntary catalog contributions, enabled iCloud sync, purchases, and separately permitted analytics and crash reporting. Your private collection is not uploaded as a whole to our Supabase catalog.

For AI research and catalog contributions, Supabase creates a technical pseudonymous session with a random user identifier when needed; session credentials are stored locally. This is not a personally registered account, but it can link requests from the same installation. A normal catalog lookup does not require a new such session. RevenueCat and Firebase use their own technical identifiers.

The legal basis for requested core functions is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; strictly necessary access to your device falls under Section 25(2)(2) of the German TDDDG. The bases for voluntary additional processing are described below.

3. Camera, photos, barcode and text recognition

The camera and system photo picker are used for captures, scans and image imports you start. Barcode and text recognition run on your device; Android uses a bundled Google ML Kit text model and iOS uses Apple Vision. Images and image crops are not sent to a recognition server for this purpose.

Temporary captures and crops are removed after completion or cancellation. Photos you keep remain local and may be transferred through enabled iCloud sync or exports you initiate. A recognised or entered barcode may subsequently be used for an online catalog lookup and, where applicable, AI research.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Camera and photo permissions can be withdrawn in system settings.

4. Online puzzle catalog using Supabase

When you scan or enter a barcode and use online lookup, the app sends the product identifier (EAN/UPC/GTIN) to our Supabase backend to find approved product data. The request does not include photos, private notes, storage locations, solving times or your entire collection.

Network access involves processing your IP address and technical connection information. The running catalog service temporarily uses the IP address for rate limiting; provider infrastructure logs may contain further technical metadata.

The provider is Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore, with its infrastructure providers and subprocessors. See Supabase – Privacy Policy. The legal basis for lookup is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; operational security and abuse prevention rely on Article 6(1)(f). Our legitimate interest is a secure and reliably available catalog.

5. AI research using OpenAI and web search

If the catalog has no match for a valid barcode and AI is available, the app may automatically follow up with AI research. This also applies to batch scanning. There is no additional confirmation before each search. You can enter details manually if AI is unavailable or a technical error occurs.

Supabase forwards only the product identifier (GTIN) and app language as user inputs to the OpenAI API to research publicly available product information. OpenAI may use its web search and associated search providers; search terms may include the barcode. Our AI integration does not forward photos, crops, recognised free text, private notes, storage locations, solving times, your private collection, your Supabase user identifier or your device IP address to OpenAI. OpenAI receives the technical connection from our backend.

The provider for EEA customers is OpenAI Ireland Ltd., Ireland; OpenAI companies and subprocessors, including in the United States, may be involved. The feature produces editable and potentially incomplete or incorrect suggestions for titles, brands, piece counts, dimensions and other product details. It does not make automated decisions about people or build personality profiles.

The API request uses store: false. This does not mean there is no retention: OpenAI states that abuse-monitoring logs may normally be kept for up to 30 days, or longer where legally required. API data is not used for model training by default according to OpenAI; explicit data-sharing opt-in would be an exception. Web search may involve further processing by search providers. See OpenAI – Data controls.

For research and abuse prevention, Supabase stores the GTIN, app language, pseudonymous user identifier, a hash of the IP address, timestamps, result status, product suggestion and token usage. The IP hash is a pseudonymous identifier, not guaranteed anonymisation. New source URLs and quotations are used only during research to check plausibility and removed before saving the suggestion and returning it to the app.

A suggestion may be reused from cache for seven days, an empty result for 24 hours and a technical error for 60 seconds. These are reuse periods, not automatic log-deletion periods. The current process does not automatically delete saved research attempts when the cache expires. They remain until separately deleted; retention criteria are abuse prevention, investigating errors and cost control. The necessary duration must be reviewed regularly and personal associations that are no longer needed must be removed.

The legal basis for requested product research is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; abuse prevention, technical error investigation and cost control rely on Article 6(1)(f). AI research is independent of voluntary catalog contributions.

6. Voluntary catalog contributions and corrections

Only with your separate consent may the app submit product details to the shared catalog when you save or change a puzzle. These include GTIN, titles and title languages, brand, artist, series, article number, product market, and the quantity, piece count and dimensions of included puzzles. Confirmed or corrected AI suggestions may be sent as ordinary contributions.

Processing also involves the technical Supabase user identifier, app language, changed fields, revision/request identifiers and timestamps. Technical access information and IP hashes are used to limit submissions. Contributions exclude images, private notes, storage locations, ownership status, solving times and sales data. Please do not put personal information into product fields.

Contributions are reviewed before approval. Approved product facts may become available to all catalog users. You can withdraw “Contribute catalog data” in Settings. This removes unsent local proposals and requests deletion of still-pending server contributions associated with that session. If the connection fails, cleanup is retried later.

Withdrawal does not automatically delete already reviewed or rejected submissions, their review records, or the technical session. Published non-personal product facts may remain in the catalog. Your statutory rights apply to personal associations and further deletion requests; please contact us. Retention of review records depends on the necessary review and traceability of corrections and the handling of specific abuse or legal cases.

The legal basis for voluntary submission is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, for device access requiring consent, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Withdrawal applies to the future. Necessary security and accountability records rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can use the local collection without consenting.

7. Optional iCloud sync and cloud backups

On supported Apple devices, you can explicitly enable Cloud Sync. Puzzle data, images, solving sessions and selected settings are then stored in the private CloudKit database of your Apple/iCloud account and synchronised between devices using that account. This is separate from Supabase and the shared product catalog. We do not provide a public CloudKit collection or gain access to your private database through this feature.

Cloud backups are separate snapshots that may contain the same collection content. After successfully creating a backup, the app attempts to retain only the three newest snapshots. Deletion markers are considered until cleanup after 180 days to support reliable synchronisation.

Disabling sync or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete existing cloud data or backups. Use the app deletion functions or iCloud management. Separately exported files must be deleted at their storage location. The provider is Apple; Apple account and iCloud processing is also subject to Apple’s terms. The legal basis for our requested sync/backup function is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

8. Voluntary usage analytics with Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics starts disabled and is enabled only after separate consent. You can change your choice in Settings at any time. App functions remain available without analytics.

Data includes a pseudonymous app-instance identifier, technical app/device/session details, and categorised screen views and interaction events. These cover onboarding, single/batch capture, barcode and OCR flows, catalog matches, saving and status changes, logbook use, filters and sorting, imports/exports, backups and cloud sync, purchase screens and purchase outcomes. Values include fixed categories, counts and, for purchases, product identifier, price and currency.

The app does not send puzzle titles, brands, GTINs, article numbers, search text, photos, private notes, storage locations, exact solving times or export content for analytics. It does not set its own user ID. Advertising ID collection and ad-personalisation signals are disabled; we do not use this analytics for advertising. IP addresses are technically processed during transmission.

The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, involving Google LLC and affiliated companies, including in the United States. See Firebase – Privacy and Security and Google. The legal basis is consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG). Withdrawal stops future collection but does not automatically erase previously sent data. User- and event-level retention depends on the Firebase/Analytics property configuration; aggregated reports may remain longer. Contact us for the currently configured period.

9. Voluntary crash reports with Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics is enabled separately from Analytics and only with your express consent, which you can withdraw in Settings. The app removes unsent reports when you decline or withdraw and before a fresh opt-in, to avoid later uploading reports created before consent.

Reports may include error type, message, stack trace, timestamp, app/OS version, device model and technical installation identifiers. The app adds no custom user ID or collection fields. Error messages may nevertheless contain technical context; we do not guarantee that every error text is entirely free of content.

The provider is Google/Firebase as in section 8. Firebase states that crash stack traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. See Firebase – Privacy and Security. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG.

10. Premium, store purchases and RevenueCat

Depending on the store offering, Premium may be purchased as a monthly subscription, annual subscription or one-time Lifetime purchase. We use RevenueCat, Inc., United States, and the relevant store to display, validate and restore entitlements. The free limit covers ten stored puzzles in total, including wishlist, completed and sold entries.

RevenueCat processes an automatically generated pseudonymous App User ID, technical device/app information, and store, product identifier, receipt or purchase token, purchase timestamps, subscription term, renewal/expiry information and entitlement status. We do not send a custom account ID, email address or collection content. See RevenueCat – Privacy Policy. Processing may take place in the United States.

Payment is handled by Apple or Google. We do not receive full card or bank details. Legal bases are Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for offers and contract performance and Article 6(1)(f) for purchase verification and abuse prevention. Retention depends on providing and restoring the purchased entitlement, resolving purchase disputes and statutory recordkeeping; subscription expiry or uninstalling the app does not automatically erase purchase records.

11. Imports, exports, sharing and store reviews

Excel files, PDFs, images, sale text and local backups are processed or created on your device. Selected content is passed to another app, recipient, clipboard or cloud service only when you initiate sharing/copying or choose a destination. Those recipients process data under their own terms. Deleting a puzzle does not automatically remove these files.

The app may request the system store-review dialog. The store decides whether to display it and processes any review submitted. The app does not learn whether or how you rated it. The legal basis for transfer functions you request is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

12. Retention, deletion and permissions

Local content remains until you delete it in the app or remove local app data. Uninstalling does not erase iCloud, Supabase, Firebase, RevenueCat or store records or files already exported. Accidentally deleted entries may temporarily remain available for Undo. Operating-system backups depend on your device settings.

The relevant sections describe external data. There is currently no general function that deletes all technical identifiers and logs across every service at once. For further requests, contact us preferably before uninstalling so association with the technical session may still be possible. We do not ask for additional identity information when it is unnecessary to handle a request.

Camera, photo selection and file access support the functions you start. Internet access is needed particularly for catalog lookup, AI, contributions, iCloud, purchase verification and enabled diagnostic services. Withholding necessary permissions or data limits that function; local manual collection management generally remains possible.

13. Recipients and processing outside the EEA

Depending on use, recipients include Supabase, OpenAI and web-search providers, Google/Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple and Google as store operators, and file/cloud recipients you select. Service providers may act as processors; store and account services also process data under their own responsibility.

Processing outside the European Economic Area, especially in the United States and Singapore, is possible. Transfers require the conditions of Articles 44 onwards GDPR, such as an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards including EU Standard Contractual Clauses. General contractual information is available from Supabase, OpenAI, Firebase and RevenueCat. These general materials do not promise exclusively European processing. Details and a copy of the safeguards applicable to a particular transfer can be requested using the contact above.

14. Your rights and withdrawal

Subject to legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and complaint to a data-protection supervisory authority. The authority for private providers at our location is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision; you may also contact another competent authority.

For processing based on legitimate interests, you may object for reasons relating to your particular situation (Article 21 GDPR). Consent for analytics, crash reports and catalog contributions can be withdrawn independently in Settings with future effect. Previous lawful processing remains unaffected.

You manage local and private iCloud content yourself; for data processed by our service providers, contact sdnightshiftstudio@gmail.com. Handling a request may require a technical user identifier even though we do not know your name.

15. Minors and changes

The app is not specifically directed at children. For consent-based functions, users under 16 need the required parental/guardian authorisation unless applicable law sets a different threshold. Purchases are subject to statutory representation rules and store requirements.

We update this policy when functions, services or legal requirements change. A new version does not replace required consent; we provide separate information about new processing requiring consent.
