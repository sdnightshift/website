Smarte BeetIdeen

Smarte BeetIdeen is a garden app in development for bed planning and plant care. Plan beds from your own photos, explore plant suggestions and care guides, and get AI-assisted pointers to possible plant problems.

In development. The features described here reflect the planned scope.

Planned features

Bed planning

Photograph your garden area, choose your style, and let our AI create realistic before-and-after images including a matching plant list.

Your digital garden

Save your projects and get access at any time to detailed care guides for watering, fertilizing, and finding the perfect spot for your plants.

Smart plant doctor

Photograph a plant problem to get AI-assisted pointers to possible causes and suggestions for relevant care steps.

Questions about Smarte BeetIdeen

Features, availability and privacy at a glance.

Is Smarte BeetIdeen available yet?

Smarte BeetIdeen is currently in development. The features described here provide a preview of the planned app. Once it is released, the relevant store link will appear on this page.

How will garden bed planning work?

Photograph an area of your garden and choose a design style. The planned AI tools will create before-and-after visualisations and a matching plant list, so you can explore design ideas using your own garden as a starting point.

Which plant care features are planned?

Saved garden projects are planned to include guidance on watering, fertilising and plant location. The plant doctor will use a photo to suggest possible causes of plant problems and relevant care steps.

Will the AI features need an internet connection?

Image generation and plant analysis are planned to use external AI services, which receive the selected photos. The Smarte BeetIdeen privacy policy explains the processing of images, local projects and optional cloud synchronisation.
