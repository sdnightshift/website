App Privacy Policy

BrainEase – Last updated: September 21, 2026

Protecting your private thoughts and personal data is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected in the "BrainEase" app, how we protect it, and what rights you have.

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for data processing in the "BrainEase" app is SD Nightshift Studio. You can find the controller's full contact details in our Legal Notice.

2. Principle: local & encrypted storage

BrainEase is developed according to the "privacy by design" principle. Your journal entries, gratitude notes, and other journal content are, by default, strongly encrypted (database encryption) and stored exclusively locally on your device. The corresponding key is stored in your device's secure system storage (Keychain / Keystore).

We have no access to your stored history data. If you want to create a backup, you can manually export your data as an additionally password-encrypted file and store it in a location of your choice. On iOS, this encrypted backup can also be manually stored in and restored from the iCloud account you use with Apple. There is no automatic cloud synchronization via our servers, and the backup password is not transmitted to us separately.

Legal basis: Local processing is carried out to provide the core function of the app that you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3. No AI analysis and no transfer of journal content

BrainEase does not provide AI analysis, automatic sorting, or voice-based feedback. Your journal content is not sent to a BrainEase server and is not processed by an AI model.

Local processing is carried out to provide the core app function you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. No user account & local profile data

BrainEase does not require registration, sign-in, or a user account. There is no server-side profile management.

A display name chosen by you, a gender selection, your reminder time, and other settings are stored exclusively on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Legal basis: Performance of the contract to provide the local features you choose (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. One-time purchase and unlock

For the one-time "BrainEase Lifetime" purchase and restoring the unlock, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) as a technical intermediary. An anonymous SDK user identifier, product and purchase status, and purchase receipts may be processed. Neither we nor RevenueCat receive your credit card or bank details; payment is handled through the Apple App Store.

Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy policies apply in addition. This is not a subscription.

Legal basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Analytics data & crash reports (opt-in only)

To find bugs and improve the app, we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google). These services are disabled by default.

Only if you expressly grant permission (opt-in) in the consent dialog may we process technical events, screen views, app and device information, and crash diagnostics. Journal text, thoughts, gratitude, wishes, moods, names, and profile data are not sent to these services. You can withdraw this consent at any time with future effect in the app settings.

Legal basis: Your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal remains unaffected by the withdrawal.

7. Permissions and device features

  • Notifications: The app only needs this permission if you want to enable local, daily reminders for your evening routine. These notifications are scheduled and triggered purely locally on your device.
  • File access (backup import & custom audio files): When you import a backup or select a custom audio file as background music, the app accesses, via your operating system's file picker, only the specific file you explicitly select. The app does not access your camera, photo library, or location; any corresponding permission prompts on iOS are shown solely due to technical dependencies of the system library used.
  • iCloud backup (iOS): If you explicitly save a backup to or restore one from iCloud, Apple processes the encrypted backup file you select under Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Feedback & support

If you send us feedback through the app, you open a form provided by our service provider Tally. The app version is passed to the form as technical context. Any other information is processed only if you enter it yourself in the form or in an email.

Legal basis: Legitimate interest in responding to inquiries and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Data transfer to third countries

Depending on how you use the app, technical data may be processed by Apple (App Store and iCloud backups), RevenueCat (purchase status), Google Firebase (only with opt-in for Analytics and Crashlytics), or Tally (feedback). Processing outside the European Economic Area cannot be ruled out.

The privacy information and appropriate safeguards of the respective providers apply to these transfers. Journal content is not sent to these services.

10. Storage period

Your journal entries and local settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Exported backup files and backups stored in iCloud remain there until you delete them yourself. Optional technical analytics and crash data, as well as purchase and feedback data, are processed according to the retention rules of the respective providers.

11. Your rights (GDPR) & data deletion

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have — where the statutory requirements are met — the following rights: right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection to processing (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent given at any time with future effect.

Since we do not store journal content or user accounts on our servers, these rights regarding your journal data lie primarily in your own hands. You can withdraw consent for technical telemetry in the app.

Data deletion: You can delete entries in the app or uninstall it to remove local app data. Exported files and iCloud backups must be deleted separately. An App Store purchase, technical provider records, or feedback you submitted are not automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Right to complain: You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your data violates the GDPR.

If you have any questions about data protection, feel free to contact us at any time. Contact details can be found in the Legal Notice.

12. Age rating and minors

BrainEase has an age rating of 4+ in the Apple App Store and is not specifically directed at children. Minors are subject to the legal requirements of their country; paid purchases may only be made with the consent of a legal guardian.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy so that it always complies with current legal requirements or to implement changes to our services. The version available at the time of your use applies. The date above ("last updated") indicates the time of the last update.
