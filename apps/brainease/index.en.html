BrainEase

BrainEase gives your thoughts a calm place to land. Write down what is on your mind, track your mood, and close the day with a personal wind-down routine. Discover also offers practical perspectives on thoughts and emotions, habits and everyday life, self-care, relationships and change, sleep and recovery, creativity, and new ways of looking at things.

View on the App Store

Age rating in the Apple App Store: 4+

What you can expect

Write it down

Write your thoughts, gratitude, and wishes down yourself. BrainEase stays intentionally manual and gives your evening a clear, personal place to land.

A calmer close

Finish your routine with a short breathing pause and soothing sounds. You can schedule a daily reminder directly on your device.

Explore new perspectives

Explore practical content across different topics, use interactive reflections, track what you have read, and listen with the optional read-aloud feature.

Local & encrypted

Your journal content is encrypted and stored on your device. Backups are started by you; optional technical telemetry never includes journal text or profile data.
